# UC Page Glossary Candidates — DHA PHA Domain

## DD Form 3024
- **Definition**: The Department of Defense standard form for Periodic Health Assessments. A multi-part clinical and administrative assessment covering intake screening, medical record review, AI/ML risk scoring, and provider review.
- **Business Context**: The entire PARATUS pipeline exists to process DD Form 3024 data. Every element in the data dictionary maps to a section of this form.
- **Data Usage**: The `dd_3024_part` column in `gold_tagged_resources` and `gold_resource_elements` identifies which part of the form each resource belongs to (Part A, B, C1, or C2).
- **Related Terms**: PHA, IMR, MHA
- **Source**: Phase 2 Instructions PDF, Section 1

## PHA (Periodic Health Assessment)
- **Definition**: A standardized health evaluation required for all Service members, documented on DD Form 3024. Covers behavioral health screening, medical record review, AI-assisted risk scoring, and provider determination.
- **Business Context**: PARATUS is a PHA data pipeline. Each FHIR Bundle in the dataset represents one Service member's complete PHA record.
- **Data Usage**: Each `bundle_id` in the pipeline corresponds to one PHA record (one Service member).
- **Related Terms**: DD Form 3024, IMR, MHA
- **Source**: Phase 2 Instructions PDF, Section 1

## IMR (Individual Medical Readiness)
- **Definition**: A Service member's medical readiness classification determined through the PHA process. The IMR Category Determination is a provider-level decision that follows AI/ML risk scoring.
- **Business Context**: The AI-to-Provider approval chain conformance rule exists because AI-generated risk scores that reach an IMR determination must have provider approval in the audit trail.
- **Data Usage**: `IMR_DETERMINANT` is a governance tag in the `pha-governance` taxonomy. Resources tagged `AI_GENERATED` + `IMR_DETERMINANT` must have a corresponding `PROVIDER_APPROVED` resource in the same Bundle.
- **Related Terms**: PHA, HITL_GATED, PROVIDER_APPROVED
- **Source**: Phase 2 Instructions PDF, Step 5 paragraph 3

## MHA (Mental Health Assessment)
- **Definition**: The behavioral health screening component of the PHA, covering Part A intake and Part C1 AI/ML risk scoring. Includes the MHA AI/ML Risk Score and MHA AI Summarization.
- **Business Context**: MHA screening responses are Input elements; MHA AI/ML Risk Score and MHA AI Summarization are Output elements that must be generated by the pipeline.
- **Data Usage**: MHA-related resources are RiskAssessment (Part C1) and ClinicalImpression (Part C1), tagged with `PART_C1, AI_GENERATED, ADVISORY_ONLY`.
- **Related Terms**: IMR, Part C1, RiskAssessment
- **Source**: Phase 2 Instructions PDF, Section 8 (Tier 2 Scope)

## Component Type (Active / Reserve)
- **Definition**: Whether a Service member is Active Duty or Reserve component. A cross-cutting administrative element present on every PHA record.
- **Business Context**: Tier 2 records must include at least one Active and one Reserve component record. Component type is required for accessibility-parity evaluation (AOI Section 4.2).
- **Data Usage**: Stored as a cross-cutting element in the Bundle. Must be preserved through transformation.
- **Related Terms**: Unit/Command Hierarchy
- **Source**: Phase 2 Instructions PDF, Section 8

## Unit/Command Hierarchy
- **Definition**: The organizational structure linking a Service member's unit to its parent command. Represented by `Organization.partOf` references within a Bundle.
- **Business Context**: The volume set contains 5 synthetic units rolling up to 1 synthetic command. This hierarchy must resolve correctly for dashboard aggregation evaluation (AOI Section 4.2).
- **Data Usage**: `Organization.partOf` reference in the FHIR Bundle. Must be preserved through transformation. Used for unit- and command-level dashboard roll-up.
- **Related Terms**: Component Type, Organization
- **Source**: Phase 2 Instructions PDF, Step 4 paragraph 2
